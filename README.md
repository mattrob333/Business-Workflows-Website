# The Strategy Stack — powered by Instinct

> **Business frameworks, finally running.**

An interactive, award-grade website that teaches the classic business frameworks — Business
Model Canvas, Five Forces, VRIO, Theory of Constraints, RACI, and twelve more — by letting
visitors **watch them run** on real-feeling companies, then hand their outputs to each other
as one connected system. The site is the public, explorable face of the thesis in
[*The Compiler Arrives*](https://github.com/mattrob333/instinct/blob/main/docs/essays/the-compiler-arrives.md):
the frameworks were never wallpaper — they were programs waiting for a processor.

overrides every other document here, including the appendix source plan. Build order and
