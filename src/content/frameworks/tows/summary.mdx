## What it is

TOWS is the half of SWOT that does the work. SWOT collects; TOWS generates.

The mechanism is a cross. Take a strength and an opportunity and ask what becomes possible
when both are true at once — that is an SO move. Cross a strength with a threat and you get
a defence; a weakness with an opportunity, a move you cannot make yet; a weakness with a
threat, the thing you should be actively avoiding. Four quadrants of pairings, and each
minted pairing is a candidate strategy with both of its parents still attached to it.

The parentage is the point. A strategy that arrived from a TOWS cross carries its own
justification: it exists because *this* capability meets *that* condition. When the
condition changes, the strategy is automatically up for review, which is not true of an
idea that arrived in a meeting.

## When it earns its place

Run it immediately after a SWOT that was collected against evidence, and never after one
that was collected against opinion — crossing two unsupported entries produces a confident
strategy resting on nothing, and the confidence comes from the geometry rather than from
the facts.

Run it before the direction frameworks. Ansoff and Three Horizons both want a list of
candidate options to sort; TOWS is where that list should come from.

> Most pairings mint nothing, and a cross that generates something from every cell has
> stopped being an analysis.

That is the discipline the framework is usually denied. Sixteen combinations do not yield
sixteen strategies. Most pairs are simply two true statements that have nothing to do with
each other, and saying so out loud is what keeps the four or five real options from
drowning in a matrix.

What good looks like: every minted option ranked, every option carrying the assumption that
would have to hold for it to work, and at least one WT entry taken seriously — the quadrant
teams skip, because it is the one that asks what you should stop doing. The output is typed
strategic options, which is exactly what the conductor reads when it decides which of them
the business currently has the capacity to attempt.
