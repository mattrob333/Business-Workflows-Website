## What it is

PESTLE is a standing question, not an exercise: which large external forces could change
this business materially before anyone inside it notices?

Six categories — political, economic, social, technological, legal, environmental — are the
prompt, and the prompt is the least interesting part. What makes a PESTLE worth keeping is
the shape of each entry. A signal is a claim about the outside world with four things
attached: how likely it is, how hard it would land, when it would land, and which part of
the business model it would hit. A signal without those is a headline somebody read.

The output is not a list of worries. It is a set of dated signals with early-warning
indicators, and the indicators are what turn the framework from a workshop into an
instrument. "Refrigerant regulation is tightening" is a conversation. "Distributor lead
times on compliant units, checked quarterly" is something you can be wrong about in public.

## When it earns its place

Run it before the terrain frameworks, because Five Forces asks where power sits *inside* a
market and PESTLE asks what could reshape the market itself. Run it again when something
outside the building has clearly moved and nobody can agree whether it matters.

Do not run it to fill a slide. The failure mode is famous and lazy: six columns, four
bullets each, every bullet true, none of them scored, and no owner. That version has never
once changed a decision, because nothing in it can be wrong.

> A signal with no indicator behind it is a rumour with a category label.

The second failure is subtler. Teams score impact confidently and horizon vaguely, which
inverts the value: a low-impact force arriving next quarter usually deserves more attention
than a high-impact one arriving in five years, and only the horizon column tells you that.
Estimate the horizon first and the list reorders itself.

What good looks like: fewer signals than the categories suggest, each with a named source
and a date, each pointing at a specific component of the business model it would break, and
each carrying the measurement you would take to notice it early. Most of the six categories
will be empty for most companies in most years. Empty is a finding — it says the weather
over this business is coming from somewhere else.
