## What it is

Jobs to be Done replaces the question *who is our customer* with a better one: what progress
is someone trying to make, in what circumstance, when they hire this?

The unit of analysis is the struggling moment, not the person. A job has a circumstance
("the quarter closed and the numbers disagree"), a desired progress, and a set of
alternatives that already exist — including the spreadsheet, the intern, and doing nothing.
Those alternatives are competitors in the only sense that matters, and they rarely appear on
a competitive landscape slide.

The output is a set of jobs with their circumstances, the outcomes people use to judge
success, the hiring criteria, and the excerpts that support each one. Functional, emotional
and social jobs are separated, because a product can serve the functional one perfectly and
still lose to something that makes the buyer look less foolish in front of their board.

## When it earns its place

Run it when usage and satisfaction disagree — strong acquisition and weak retention, or a
feature everyone asked for and nobody opened. Run it before the value proposition work,
because a fit assessment needs something to be fit *to*.

Run it from interviews, not from personas. The evidence is what someone did the last time
they were in the circumstance, told in their words, with the timeline intact: what triggered
the search, what they tried first, what made them switch, what nearly stopped them.

> People do not buy a quarter-inch drill or a quarter-inch hole. They buy a shelf that is up
> before the in-laws arrive.

The failure mode is a job statement written by the product team. It always comes out as a
description of the product with the brand removed, and it always confirms the roadmap, which
is how you can tell it was never evidence. If no finding surprised anyone, the interviews
were leading.

What good looks like: jobs stated in the customer's language, each with frequency and
importance, each traceable to specific excerpts, and at least one job the company is
currently doing nothing about. Those become customer jobs and pains in the shared state,
which the canvas, the fit assessment and the constraint read in turn.
